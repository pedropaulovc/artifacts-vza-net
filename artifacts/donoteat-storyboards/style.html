How an episode is put together

Most videos follow the same order. A Cities: Skylines build plays on screen for nearly the whole runtime while the narration gives a history or policy lecture over it. The build illustrates the argument, and the game's cheerful feedback often argues against it.

Some Franklin episodes open with a statement about current events before the episode starts. Episode 4 opens with Abolish ICE and episode 7 with prison-strike solidarity. Episode 5 begins with a mock non-apology that ends on ABOLISH ICE filling the screen.

Narration

Viewers have compared the voice to a retiree or contractor patiently explaining why your home addition will cost ten times as much and take three times as long. The delivery stays level for statistics and punchlines alike, so a joke arrives in the same tone as the history around it.

Verbal habits

Across the 24 transcripts, he tags "right?" onto claims 204 times in 13 videos and says "folks" 85 times in 19, as in "Protectionism. It works, folks." "Anyway" (57 times in 20 videos) is how he leaves a tangent. In Banking & Commerce he checks in mid-explanation: "Are you following this? Okay, good."
