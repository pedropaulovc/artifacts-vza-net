Premise

The presenter traces Greenwood’s locally circulating wealth, the 1921 racist massacre and its recovery, arguing that later freeway and institutional redevelopment did more lasting damage by removing land from community use. He ties together racism, class power and community self-defense, and argues that prosperity offered no protection.
