Premise

A commissioned Transport Fever dome-car showcase grows into a history of metallurgy, Budd and American rail procurement. The presenter and guest Miles explain material tradeoffs and Budd’s unusually durable stainless passenger cars. They argue that shrinking demand, bespoke procurement, protectionist factory churn and rigid safety rules undermined stable domestic rail manufacturing. Miles brings industrial-design and rail history to the conversation.
