Franklin · Episode 1

Cities Skylines | Franklin, Episode 1: Prehistory

17:46 · 80 panels

  • Watch on YouTube

Premise

Donoteat rejects Cities: Skylines’ frictionless mayor-as-god model, introduces historical time through floodplain formation, and builds Lenape settlements as Franklin’s precolonial foundation, with caveats about their accuracy.
