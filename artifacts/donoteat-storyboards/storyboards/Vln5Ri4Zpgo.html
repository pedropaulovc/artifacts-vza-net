Franklin · Series intro

A new Cities: Skylines Series: Franklin

0:41 · 8 panels

  • Watch on YouTube

Premise

Donoteat announces Franklin using escalating title cards that promise a Cities: Skylines series across time, education and entertainment, then reveal the series name, creator credit and June 23 arrival date. The grand claims are trailer hype, and no city story is narrated.
