## Premise and central argument

Donoteat founds the fictional colonial city of Franklin in Cities: Skylines, using its plan to explain a lineage of rational street grids and the gap between an ideal urban design and the economy that occupies it. The presenter argues that William Franklin's spacious, tolerant “green country town” is immediately undone by port-centered mercantilist development: subdivided lots, dense row houses, unequal property relations, and coerced labor. His discussion distinguishes indentured servitude and penal transportation from hereditary chattel slavery, then makes whiskey production and consumption part of the same colonial economy. These are the presenter's historical explanations and fictionalized city-building narrative, not independently verified historical findings.
