## Premise and central argument

Donoteat founds the fictional colonial city of Franklin in Cities: Skylines, using its plan to trace rational street grids and the gap between an ideal urban design and the economy that occupies it. In his account, William Franklin’s spacious, tolerant “green country town” gives way to port commerce, subdivided lots, dense row houses, unequal property relations and coerced labor. He distinguishes indentured servitude and penal transportation from hereditary chattel slavery, then places whiskey production and consumption in the same colonial economy.
