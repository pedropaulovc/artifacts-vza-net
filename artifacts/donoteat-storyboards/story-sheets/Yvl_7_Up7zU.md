## Premise and central argument

The presenter begins with the popular internet account of Marvin Heemeyer as a reasonable man driven to armored revenge by a town that blocked his business and severed his sewer access. He challenges it through Patrick Brower’s book, annotated aerial maps and a Cities: Skylines reconstruction of Granby, Colorado. In his account, Heemeyer owned a successful business, received offers including a free sewer connection, and rejected ways to dispose of sewage properly before the rampage. The heroic-machine story becomes an engineering lesson about poop. The sweeping jokes about small-business owners are the presenter’s opinions.
