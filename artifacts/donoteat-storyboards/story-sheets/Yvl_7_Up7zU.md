## Premise and central argument

The presenter begins with the popular internet account of Marvin Heemeyer as a reasonable man driven to armored revenge by a town that blocked his business and severed his sewer access. He then dismantles that account through Patrick Brower’s book, annotated aerial maps and a Cities: Skylines reconstruction of Granby, Colorado. His argument is that Heemeyer was a successful property owner repeatedly offered accommodation, including a free sewer connection, whose inflated land demands and refusal to dispose of sewage properly preceded his rampage. The episode deliberately replaces a heroic machine narrative with an engineering and public-health story about poop. Its historical claims, evaluations of motives and sweeping jokes about small-business owners are the presenter’s, not independently verified conclusions of this sheet.
