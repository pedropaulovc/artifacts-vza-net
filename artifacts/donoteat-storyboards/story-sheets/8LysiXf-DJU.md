## Premise and central argument

Donoteat treats Cities: Skylines' Industries expansion as a “State Capitalism expansion”: a player-bureaucrat directs extraction and industrial production but cannot direct the freight system that connects them. His comic frame casts the player as an official serving Xi Jinping and proving “socialism with Chinese characteristics” to the West. He compares the game's ore, oil, forestry and farming chains with his account of their real-world counterparts, accumulating both technical nitpicks and darker labor/environmental digressions. His central criticism is not that every industrial detail must be exact, but that all-truck logistics, uncontrolled train dispatch and intermediary warehouses aggravate traffic and consume limited simulation agents. His historical, chemical, engineering and agricultural assertions below are attributed to the presenter, not independently verified by this sheet.
