## Premise and central argument

Donoteat treats Cities: Skylines' Industries expansion as a “State Capitalism expansion”: a player-bureaucrat directs extraction and production but cannot control the freight system that connects them. He casts the player as an official serving Xi Jinping and proving “socialism with Chinese characteristics” to the West. The tour compares the game's ore, oil, forestry and farming chains with his account of real industry, with detours into machinery, chemistry, labor and environmental damage. His main complaint is the logistics: all-truck transfers, uncontrolled train dispatch and intermediary warehouses worsen traffic and consume limited simulation agents.
