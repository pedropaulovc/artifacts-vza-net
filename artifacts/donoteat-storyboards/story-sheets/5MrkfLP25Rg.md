## Premise and central argument

Donoteat01 builds Franklin’s Good Hill Water Works while explaining how municipal water supply evolved from buckets and wells to aqueducts and pumped reservoirs. He models the installation on Philadelphia’s Fairmount Water Works, naming the inspiration at the end. In his account, relatively clean, inexpensive infrastructure still delivers its immediate benefits unequally: subscriptions and construction charges favor prosperous households, while long working hours leave the poor little time for the pleasure grounds. He treats the waterworks as an overall improvement despite environmental damage and the long wait for public-health and development benefits to “trickle down.”
