## Premise and central argument

The presenter develops three Scandinavian colonial forts and trading posts, Fort Sven, Fort Jens, and Fort Hans, on the future Franklin map, using a fictional “New Norway” loosely based on New Sweden. The building demonstration carries an explanation of mercantilism: the presenter argues that zero-sum trade ideology, bullion accumulation, colonial resource extraction, and subsistence wages formed an interconnected system that enriched colonial powers at others’ expense. He then argues that the logic of keeping essential workers economically precarious survives in modern wage hierarchies. The historical and political arguments are his own, and the colony and its settlements are explicitly fictional.
