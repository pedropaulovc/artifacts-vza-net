## Premise and central argument

Donoteat opens with a deliberately glib conventional Cities: Skylines build, using the game's frictionless infrastructure, zoning, demolition, and opaque finances to argue that its mayor-as-god model misses how cities arise. His alternative Franklin series will introduce historical time and competing interests: first forming a floodplain landscape, then placing settlements representing Lenape communities before European colonization. The geography, cultural descriptions, and historical comparisons below are the presenter's assertions, not independently verified historical findings. Crucially, he explicitly corrects his own portrayal of Lenape subsistence and disclaims historical expertise about village layouts.
