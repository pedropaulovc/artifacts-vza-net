## Premise and central argument

Donoteat01 uses the creation and placement of an Eastern State Penitentiary asset to challenge *Cities: Skylines*' uncomplicated model of crime, policing, punishment, and rehabilitation. His subject is not yet modern American policing, but prison architecture as social control. Moving from ancient punishment through Enlightenment prison reform and an extended architectural detour, he argues that Eastern State's supposedly humane Pennsylvania/separate system tried to manufacture penitence through isolation, surveillance, and compulsory work. Its architectural ideal failed under prisoner resistance, overcrowding, and the economics of competing labor systems. He then argues that modern incarceration preserves some of its coercive labor mechanisms in a much more punitive capitalist form. These are the presenter's historical and political claims, not independently verified findings of this sheet.
