Frames and dialogue

The collection contains 24 selected videos, totaling 12 hours, 28 minutes and 43 seconds. Whisper large-v3 produced the first transcripts. Every transcribed word is assigned to a panel, and the full dialogue remains searchable.

An AI assistant reviewed sampled frames and contact sheets, selected panels and followed visual-gag setups and payoffs. Targeted rereads added frames for brief inserts. The sampled frames were not a continuous, human-verified viewing of the collection. Short overlays and effects can still be missed.

Pedro Paulo Vezza Campos located several visual and audio gags missed by the initial annotations and audio model. His corrections include Big Chungus in the prison image, the repeated Law & Order stings and the students booing at the SOCIAL DEMOCRATS card. He also corrected transcription errors and identified cultural references.

Reviewed transcript corrections change the displayed words and search text while leaving the original transcript files intact. The correction files below preserve the original wording, replacement and viewer evidence. The dialogue is still a machine transcript, with possible errors outside those reviewed corrections.

Original working notes (JSON) preserve the earlier board prose and story sheets.

The audio pass

A later local pass used Qwen2.5-Omni-7B on an RTX 3090 to listen for music, effects and delivery. It processed 40-second excerpts with five seconds of overlap. The current evidence records 24 videos, 1293 of 1293 valid excerpts and 0 processing errors. The excerpts cover each source without gaps.

Zero processing errors means the scheduled input ran successfully. It does not mean the model found every gag or identified every sound correctly. Calibration did not reliably identify known Law & Order stings, even in shorter follow-up excerpts. Viewer-confirmed annotations take precedence.

The raw model responses remain linked below. Their observations are unreviewed candidates and do not replace dialogue, accepted gags or soundtrack identities. A 40-second excerpt gives broad context, not an exact effect onset or a continuous cue. An empty event list does not establish silence.
